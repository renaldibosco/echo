# Echo 2.0 (mobile)

Paul's Echo assistant for Android. It thinks with Google Gemini, and speech recognition and text-to-speech run natively on the phone.

**Download:** https://github.com/renaldibosco/echo/releases/latest/download/Echo.apk

## What it can do
- **Talk:** tap the orb, type, use the home-screen widget, say "Hey Echo" (in the app or with the screen off), or long-press Home once Echo is your assistant
- **Apps and media:** "open WhatsApp", "play Anirudh songs", "play … on Spotify", "search for …", "research …"
- **Calls and messages:** "call Amma", "call 98765 43210", "message Tharun I'm on the way" (WhatsApp), "text Appa …" (SMS)
- **Reminders and alarms:** "remind me in 20 minutes to study", "remind me tomorrow at 7 am to …", "set an alarm for 6 30 AM", "set a timer for 5 minutes"
- **Memory:** "remember that my exam is on October 14", "what do you remember", "forget …"
- **Photos:** "what's this?" or the camera button. Gemini explains diagrams and solves questions
- **Study:** "quiz me on enzymes", then "stop quiz"
- **Tamil:** "speak in Tamil" / "speak in English"
- **Phone control:** volume, brightness, silent/vibrate, battery, torch, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth panels
- **Notifications:** "read my notifications"
- **Markets:** "any BOF signals", "price of Nifty" (same engine as Reno's BOF)
- **Laptop link:** "laptop open vs code" runs on desktop Echo over Wi-Fi
